About the Event

Think you need to be a coding expert to build something cool? Think again.

Build with AI: ABC (Anybody Can Code) is a beginner-friendly, hands-on event where students turn ideas into real applications using AI and Google technologies.

There is no competition here, no judging pressure, and no prerequisites -- just creativity, innovation, and building together in a welcoming campus space.

Don't hesitate. Don't overthink. Just show up, explore, build and learn.

Technologies

Tools You Will Explore

Get hands-on experience with modern AI and developer tools from Google.

Google AI Studio

Prototype prompts, test Gemini models, and turn ideas into working AI workflows in minutes.

Google Stitch

Design clean user interfaces and convert concepts into structured layouts effortlessly.

Google Antigravity

Build, iterate, and ship applications with an AI-assisted agentic development environment.

Android Development Tools

Explore modern mobile app creation and smart tooling for Android devices.

Google Cloud

Host, deploy, and scale your applications reliably on cloud infrastructure.

Workshop Flow

What You Will Do

Four guided steps from an everyday idea to a working application.

  1. Step 01

    Understand a problem statement

    Pick a real-world campus or everyday problem and break it down into clear, solvable parts.

  2. Step 02

    Explore AI-powered tools

    Learn how AI Studio, Stitch, Antigravity, and Google Cloud fit together to accelerate creation.

  3. Step 03

    Build your application

    Work hands-on with mentors nearby to design, generate, and refine your own working project.

  4. Step 04

    Learn and showcase

    Share what you built with peers, exchange feedback, and leave with practical skills you can keep using.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need coding experience?
Not at all! This event is designed for beginners. Whether you have never written a line of code or are just getting started, you are welcome here.
What should I bring?
Just your laptop and curiosity. We will provide all the guidance, tools, and support you need.
Is it free?
Yes, the event is completely free for all registered participants. Registration for this session is now closed.
Is it a competition?
No, this is not a competition. It is a hands-on learning event where you explore, build, and learn at your own pace. No pressure, no judging, just building.
Can first-year students join?
Absolutely! Students from all years are welcome. First-year students are especially encouraged to participate.
Do I need a team?
No, you can participate individually. You are free to collaborate with others during the event, but there is no requirement to form a team beforehand.
